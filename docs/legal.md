# Privacy Policy

## 1. Who we are

Halo OS (“Halo,” “we”) provides an AI operating system for automotive dealers. This policy explains how we handle information on our website and in our platform.

## 2. Two kinds of information

- **Information about you as a website visitor or user:** your name, work email, phone number, company, role, and number of rooftops when you join the wait list, and how you use our website and platform.

- **Dealer data:** information our dealer customers connect to Halo, such as deal, vehicle, customer and operational data. We process it on behalf of our dealer customers, under our contracts with them. If you’re a car buyer whose information a dealer holds, contact that dealer.

## 3. How we use information

- To provide, secure and support the Halo platform

- To respond to inquiries and requests for early access

- To improve our website and platform

- To create anonymized, combined industry benchmarks that don’t identify any dealer, store or person

## 4. What we don’t do

- We don’t sell personal information or dealer data.

- We don’t share one dealer group’s data with another, or use a group’s specific operations to help another group.

- Dealer data isn’t used to train AI models. Our AI runs through Google Cloud, under terms that exclude training on customer data.

## 5. Who we share information with

Only service providers that help us run Halo, such as Google Cloud for hosting and AI processing, under contracts that limit how they use it. We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or as part of a merger or acquisition, subject to this policy.

## 6. Security

Each dealer group’s data is kept in its own isolated environment in the United States, encrypted in storage and in transit, and protected by two-factor login and role-based access. See our Security & Governance page.

## 7. Retention

We keep dealer data for the length of our contract with the dealer, then return and delete it as the contract provides. We keep website and account information as long as needed for the purposes above.

## 8. Your choices and rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, or opt out of marketing emails, by contacting david@drivehalo.ai. Depending on where you live, you may have more rights under state law.

## 9. Cookies and analytics

Our website uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, a privacy-focused tool that doesn’t use cookies, to understand how visitors use it. The site also loads fonts from Google Fonts, which shares your IP address with Google when a page loads.

## 10. Contact

david@drivehalo.ai
