## 1. Who we are and what this policy covers

This Privacy Policy explains how Halo OS (“Halo,” “we,” “us”) collects, uses, and shares personal information when you visit drivehalo.ai (the “Site”) or join our wait list.

It does not cover data that dealerships process through the Halo OS platform. That data is governed by the written agreement between Halo and each dealership, under which Halo acts on the dealership’s behalf and processes its data only as that agreement allows.

## 2. Information we collect

**Information you give us.** When you join the wait list we collect your name, dealership or group name, number of rooftops, role, work email, and phone number. If you email us, we receive whatever you choose to include.

**Information collected automatically.** Like most websites, our hosting and security providers record basic technical information when you visit, such as IP address, browser and device type, pages requested, referring page, and the date and time of the request. We use privacy-focused, cookieless analytics to understand overall traffic, such as page views and visit counts, without tracking you across other websites.

## 3. How we use information

- To review wait list requests and contact you about Halo, including scheduling a walkthrough.

- To respond to your questions and messages.

- To operate, secure, maintain, and improve the Site.

- To detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security incidents.

- To comply with legal obligations and enforce our rights.

We do not use the personal information we collect through the Site to train AI models.

## 4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We share it only:

- **With service providers** who help us run the Site and our business, such as website hosting, content delivery and security, analytics, and email. They may use it only to provide services to us.

- **With affiliated companies** under common ownership or control with Halo, for the purposes described in this policy.

- **For legal reasons**, when we believe disclosure is required by law, legal process, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Halo, our users, or others.

- **In a business transaction**, such as a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, subject to standard confidentiality protections.

- **With your consent** or at your direction.

## 5. Cookies and similar technologies

The Site does not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking pixels, and our analytics do not set cookies. We may use strictly necessary cookies to keep the Site secure and working, for example to protect forms from spam. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; parts of the Site may not work as intended if you do.

The Site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google’s servers and shares your IP address with Google when a page loads.

## 6. How long we keep information

We keep wait list and contact information for as long as needed to evaluate and follow up on your request and maintain our business relationship, and then for a reasonable period afterward unless you ask us to delete it sooner. We keep technical logs for a limited time for security and troubleshooting. We may keep information longer when the law requires it.

## 7. How we protect information

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access controls. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 8. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you, by emailing david@drivehalo.ai. Every marketing email we send includes a way to opt out.

Depending on where you live, including certain U.S. states, you may have additional rights, such as the right to know what personal information we collect, to obtain a copy, to correct it, to delete it, and to opt out of its sale or sharing (we do not sell or share it). We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request, and you may use an authorized agent where the law allows.

## 9. Children

The Site is intended for business professionals and is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

## 10. Where information is processed

Halo is based in the United States, and we process and store information in the United States. If you access the Site from outside the U.S., your information will be transferred to and processed in the U.S.

## 11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the “Last updated” date below and, if the changes are significant, provide additional notice on the Site.

## 12. Contact us

Questions or requests about this policy or your personal information: david@drivehalo.ai.
